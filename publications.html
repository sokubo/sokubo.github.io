Survey reports

With Hiroshi Ishida, Kenji Ishida, Misaki Matano and others I co-author the annual analytical reports of the Japanese Life Course Panel Surveys (JLPS) — the ISS Panel Survey Discussion Paper Series (nos. 112, 121, 128, 143, 151, 165, 174) and the two-part reports in Chūō Chōsa-hō (2019–2025) — covering health, employment, caregiving, migration, wellbeing, and life under COVID-19.
